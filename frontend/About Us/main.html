About Us

Welcome to the (App Title)! 🎉
We built this tool to simplify how you manage receipts, invoices, and financial records. No more paper piles or hours of manual entry — our app uses smart OCR technology to extract data from images and organize it for you.

Why we made this:
As students and researchers, we noticed how much time gets wasted in managing documents. Whether you're a freelancer, small business owner, or just organizing household expenses — we wanted to create something useful, clean, and stress-free.

Meet the Team:
We’re a small group of passionate developers who enjoy solving everyday problems through technology. We believe that tools should work for you, not the other way around.
